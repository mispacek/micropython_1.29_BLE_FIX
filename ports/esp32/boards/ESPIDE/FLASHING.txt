ESP32-C3 / ESP32-C6 MicroPython v1.29.0 BLE + Wi-Fi

Use the artifact for your chip. For a normal installation, select firmware.bin
and flash it at address 0x0, then reset the board. It includes the bootloader,
partition table and MicroPython application.

micropython.bin is the application ONLY. Its offset is 0x10000. It requires
the matching bootloader at 0x0 and partition table at 0x8000. Do not flash
micropython.bin at 0x0.

boot.py and main.py are separate filesystem files, not part of firmware.bin.
Reset the board to discard any temporary diagnostic monkey patches before
testing the new firmware. A complete flash erase is not required for this fix.

The ELF file is supplied for crash diagnosis and is not a flash image.
